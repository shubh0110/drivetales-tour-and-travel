🎨 ANIMATION TYPES (Quick Reference)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Page Load Animations:
  .animate-fade-in-down       Fade + slide down
  .animate-fade-in-up         Fade + slide up
  .animate-fade-in-left       Fade + slide left
  .animate-fade-in-right      Fade + slide right
  .animate-scale-in           Scale from small
  .animate-scale-in-up        Scale + bounce
  .animate-zoom-in            Zoom in
  .animate-rotate-in          Rotate in
  .animate-float              Floating motion
  .animate-float-slow         Slow floating
  .animate-pulse              Pulsing opacity
  .animate-glow               Glowing effect

Hover Effects:
  .hover-scale                Scale on hover
  .hover-lift                 Lift with shadow
  .hover-glow                 Glow effect
  .hover-fade                 Fade effect
  .hover-underline            Underline animation

Scroll Animations:
  .scroll-animate             Auto-triggers on scroll

Special Animations:
  [data-parallax="0.3"]       Parallax depth
  [data-text-reveal="word"]   Text reveal
  [data-counter="500"]        Counter animation

Delays (for staggering):
  .delay-100 through .delay-1000
